## Why this is the highest-priority request

**R-01 is the project's top risk: "school assets never arrive."** Engineering can complete every phase and the site still cannot launch without these. In projects of this type, content is reliably the slowest dependency — schools are busy, and "send us your photographs" competes with running a school.

This list is deliberately **specific and itemised** rather than an open-ended ask, because "send us some photos" is not actionable and "20–40 photographs of the following subjects" is.

**Nothing below has been invented or assumed.** Every item is currently a `[PLACEHOLDER]` token in the blueprint.

> **Section A blocks launch. Section B does not** — the site can go live without it and grow afterwards. Please do not delay Section A while gathering Section B.

> ⚠️ **The most important item in this document.** The system strips location metadata, records a consent basis, restricts uploads, and provides expedited takedown — but **it cannot know which child is on an exclusion list.** Only the school knows that. Technical controls reduce risk; your process is what actually prevents harm.
>
> If no consent process currently exists, please say so plainly. That is a finding to address, not a failure — and it must be resolved before any photograph of an identifiable child is published.

## How to send

- **Photographs:** originals, unedited, highest resolution. Do not compress or resize.
- **Documents:** editable formats (DOCX) preferred over PDF where possible.
- **Logo:** vector if it exists — ask your designer or printer if unsure.
- **Text:** plain text or a document; no formatting needed.

Partial delivery is genuinely useful — **please send Section A items as they become available rather than waiting to complete the whole list.** Identity and photography (A1, A12) unblock the most work.
